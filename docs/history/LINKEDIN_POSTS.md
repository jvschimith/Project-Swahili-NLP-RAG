## Post 3: Educational & Community Audience (General + Linguists)

🗣️ Ever wondered how AI understands Swahili? I just built a system that breaks it down!

Swahili is one of Africa's most widely spoken languages, with fascinating linguistic features like the Bantu noun class system. I created an interactive tool that anyone can use to analyze Swahili text!

🎓 Cool things it can do:

1️⃣ **Sentiment Analysis**
Paste a Swahili news article and instantly see if it's positive, negative, or neutral - with confidence scores!

2️⃣ **Keyword Extraction**
Automatically identifies the most important words and topics in any text. Perfect for understanding what Swahili news is really about.

3️⃣ **Bantu Classification**
Type a Swahili word like "kitabu" (book) → Get its Bantu class (KI-VI - Tools/Artifacts) with examples!

4️⃣ **Morphology Analysis**
Break down complex words into roots and affixes:
"ninakupenda" → prefix: "ninaku" + root: "pend" + suffix: "a"

📊 Interactive Dashboard:
Just paste a URL from BBC Swahili, VOA, or any Swahili news site and see:
• Beautiful charts showing sentiment trends
• Top 10 keywords with frequencies
• Vocabulary richness scores
• Word distribution pie charts

🌟 Why this matters:
Most NLP tools focus on English, Spanish, or Chinese. African languages deserve the same level of technology! This project makes advanced language analysis accessible for:

🔬 Built with:
