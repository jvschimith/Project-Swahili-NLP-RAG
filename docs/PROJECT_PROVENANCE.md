# Project Provenance

## Origin

Swahili Linguistic Intelligence originated in Brazil as an independent academic and
software-engineering effort spanning computational linguistics, low-resource NLP,
morphology, corpus engineering, public-web data acquisition, data engineering, and
reproducible research software.

## Language Policy

- **English** is the language of engineering, international technical documentation,
  APIs, contributor communication, and scientific publication.
- **Portuguese** is the author's native language and records the project's Brazilian
  scientific and technological provenance.
- **Swahili** is the object language of study and is preserved in linguistic examples,
  lexica, corpus material, morphology, inputs, and evaluation data.

## Scientific Provenance

International publication in English improves accessibility but must not erase the
project's geographic, academic, and intellectual origin. Brazilian authorship is part
of the research provenance, just as source and corpus provenance are part of
reproducibility.

> Este projeto foi concebido e desenvolvido no Brasil por um desenvolvedor e
> pesquisador brasileiro, falante nativo de português. O uso do inglês para comunicação
> internacional preserva sua origem brasileira e seu compromisso com a soberania
> científica e tecnológica nacional.

## Scientific and Technological Sovereignty

The repository deliberately recognizes Brazilian scientific authorship and software
production while remaining open to inspection, reproducible research, and international
collaboration. Here, sovereignty means retaining transparent intellectual provenance
and local technical capacity, without limiting exchange or overstating institutional
claims.
